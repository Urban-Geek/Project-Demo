🗓️ Upcoming Community Events

Community events are a great way to feel connected beyond the classroom. They bring students, families, and staff together to celebrate learning, culture, talents, and achievements.

For students with disabilities, community events also create opportunities to build confidence, practice social skills, and discover new interests. Whether it’s a school showcase, an awareness event, a family night, a club meeting, or a volunteer opportunity, these experiences help strengthen a sense of belonging and leadership.

We encourage all students to participate in ways that feel comfortable and meaningful. Your voice, talents, and presence matter in our community.

💼 Scholarships & Funding for Students

Planning for life after high school can feel overwhelming—especially when thinking about how to pay for college, trade school, or certification programs. The good news is that scholarships and funding opportunities are available, including support designed specifically for students with disabilities.

If you have an IEP or 504 Plan, you may qualify for state, federal, or community‑based financial support. Some programs offer help with tuition, books, transportation, assistive technology, and career training.

Scholarships are not just based on grades. Many recognize leadership, persistence, personal growth, and future goals. Your journey, strengths, and resilience matter.

Start early, ask questions, and connect with your school counselor or case manager. There are supports in place to help you take the next step toward your future. Your education is possible. Your goals are within reach.

🎓 High School

🎓 High School Success Hub

High school is a new chapter filled with classes, opportunities, and responsibilities. It can feel exciting, challenging, and sometimes overwhelming — and that’s completely normal.

If you have an IEP or 504 Plan, your supports are here to help you succeed. High school is a place where you will grow, discover your strengths, and begin preparing for your future.

You belong here, and this journey is yours.

🌱 9th Grade: Start Strong

High school is a new beginning. This year is about learning how high school works and building habits that help you stay organized and confident.

  • Understanding your accommodations
  • Using a planner or tracking system
  • Asking for help early
  • Getting involved in a club or activity

🔭 10th Grade: Build Confidence

You’ve made it through freshman year. Now it’s time to build independence and start thinking more about your interests and goals.

  • Using supports without reminders
  • Checking your grades regularly
  • Exploring career interests
  • Setting academic goals

📚 11th Grade: Plan Your Path

Junior year is an important planning year. College, trade school, military service, or entering the workforce are all valid options.

  • Meeting graduation requirements
  • Researching postsecondary programs
  • Learning how accommodations work after high school
  • Practicing professional communication

🛣️12th Grade: Finish Strong

Senior year is about stepping into your future with confidence and understanding how to access support after graduation.

  • Completing applications (college, trade school, jobs)
  • Connecting with disability services (if attending college)
  • Finalizing your graduation plan
  • Continuing to advocate for yourself

🦸 Super Learners Corner (Pre‑K – 5th)

Welcome to Super Learners Corner!

Every child learns in their own special way, and that’s what makes school amazing. Some learners use pictures, some use movement, some use extra time, and some use tools that help their brains focus. All of these are smart ways to learn.

Here, you can explore fun topics, watch videos, and discover strategies that help you shine. You’ll learn about friendship, feelings, using your tools, and celebrating your strengths.
